Ask This Video

A Chrome extension that lets you chat with any YouTube video. Ask a question and get an answer from what's actually said in it, with timestamps you can click to jump there.

Try it (beta)

  1. Download the extension zip I sent you and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped extension folder.
  4. Open any YouTube video and click Ask this video in the bottom-right corner.

What I'd like feedback on

  2. Did it fail on any video? Please send me the link.
  3. Was anything confusing or slow?
